Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I download a YouTube thumbnail using Thumbrio?

Simply copy the YouTube video URL, paste it into the input field on Thumbrio, click 'Extract', choose your desired quality, and then click 'Download Thumbnail'.

Is Thumbrio free to use?

Yes, Thumbrio is completely free to use for downloading YouTube video thumbnails.

What thumbnail qualities can I download?

Thumbrio allows you to download YouTube thumbnails in various qualities including Low, Normal, HD, 1080p, and even 4K and 8K resolutions when available.

Is it legal to download YouTube thumbnails?

Downloading publicly available YouTube thumbnails for personal or educational use is generally considered legal. However, for commercial use or redistribution, it's advisable to obtain permission from the original creator to avoid copyright issues.

Do I need to install any software to use Thumbrio?

No, Thumbrio is an online web application. You can use it directly from your browser without needing to install any software.
